## Context
M2 importers, validation, desurvey and compositing (and later estimation) need
drillhole datasets whose correct answers are known. The generator writes field-
style CSV files plus a truth manifest. It is a development tool, not shipped.

## Required behaviour
- `generate(config) -> Dataset` and `write(dataset, out_dir, dialect, header_style)`;
  CLI `python tools/synth_drillholes.py --seed N --holes N --out DIR [--dialect]
  [--headers] [--errors none|standard|all]`. Deterministic for a seed (same
  platform: byte-identical files).
- Geology from parameters stored in the manifest: analytic topography; a
  tabular ore zone between two parallel planes (dip direction, dip, true
  thickness); lithology OB (overburden), AND (hanging wall), QV (ore zone),
  SCH (footwall); log-normal Au (ppm) and correlated Cu (%) inside/outside.
- Holes: collars on a jittered grid on the topography; DD holes with survey
  stations every ~30 m and smooth random build/turn; ~10 % vertical; RC holes
  with a collar survey only. Depth passes the footwall by 20–60 m.
- Samples 1 m (RC 2 m) split at lithology contacts (min 0.3 m), starting below
  overburden. Contacts found on the true trace to 0.001 m.
- Minimum-curvature desurvey implemented in the tool; it must reproduce
  `tests/reference/desurvey/cases.csv` within 1e-6 m (test required).
- Truth outputs: `truth.json` (config, geology, clean collars/surveys) and
  `samples_truth.csv` (hole_id, from, to, x, y, z of midpoint, inside, true
  grades); `issues.json`: every injected defect or special value with code,
  file, 1-based line, hole_id, detail.
- Injected cases (`standard` = all at low rate): overlap, from>=to, interval
  beyond total depth, duplicate collar, assay hole without collar, survey
  beyond depth, azimuth 365 / -10, |dip| > 90, single upward (sign-flipped)
  station, duplicate survey depth with different values, non-numeric text,
  unknown lithology code, hole-id whitespace/case variants; special values
  (not errors) "", "-", "NS", "IS", "LNR", "<0.005", "-0.005".
- Dialects: `std` (UTF-8, `,`, `.`, LF), `tr` (UTF-8 BOM, `;`, decimal comma,
  CRLF), `tr1254` (Windows-1254, `;`, decimal comma, CRLF). Header styles
  `canonical`, `short`, `turkish` exactly as listed in the prompt.

## Edge cases
Holes that miss the ore zone; holes collared inside it; contacts within 0.3 m
of a sample boundary; azimuth wrap through north.

## Out of scope
XLSX output, block models, real data, any change outside `allowed_paths`.

## Report back
Files, commands run with real output, timing for 500 holes, open questions.
